Scope, matching the implemented checkpoint exactly: records a Loan or Eid Advance and tracks its remaining balance; at most one ACTIVE advance per employee per type (approved, frozen decision — a partial unique index backstops an application-layer check); automatic deduction materialization at new-cycle creation via a direct call from payroll-processing.service.ts into advances.service.ts — deliberately not a generic Outstanding-Payroll-Obligation provider/hook registry (approved architecture decision); schedule deferral to any future Draft-eligible period, with a complete append-only AdvanceScheduleChange history. No new permission — advances:manage already existed, reused unchanged, granted only to Payroll Staff and Master Admin; Finance receives none. Payroll Entry CSV/Excel import/export is unchanged — no automatic Advance linking during import. Cash Advances, Advance-only Bank Sheets, and Company Bank Account management are explicitly out of scope and not depicted here. Employee Statements, Reports, Dashboard, and any Phase 5 functionality are equally out of scope.

Historical integrity: once a Payroll Entry releases, its advanceDeduction/advanceId (or eid- equivalent) freeze permanently via the same assertEntryEditable guard every other field already obeys — verified directly in backend/tests/advances.test.ts: editing or deferring an Advance after its entry has released never modifies the released row. Deferral can reverse a FULL_DEDUCTION advance's just-materialized PAID_OFF status back to ACTIVE while its entry is still Draft — nothing about a not-yet-released deduction is ever final.
